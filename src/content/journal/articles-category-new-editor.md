## New Editor!

Rev. Angus Stewart

This is the first publication of the British Reformed Journal (BRJ) under its sixth editor. The first editor, Tony Horne, was an Englishman who later moved to Scotland. The second, Lindsay Williams, is a Welshman who lives in England. The third editor, Michael Kimmitt, was an Englishman who lived in Wales. The fourth, Allen Baird, has lived in N. Ireland all his days. The fifth, Rev. Martyn McGeown, is an Ulsterman who was serving as a missionary in the Republic of Ireland (and is now in America, having accepted a call to pastor a church in Michigan). The sixth, Samuel Watterson, has spent most of his life in the Republic of Ireland and is now in Northern Ireland. All the five major parts of the British Isles are represented, in one way or another, by our BRJ editors!

With the last three men, our editors have been getting younger. Sam's German wife, Anganeta, recently gave birth to their fifth child, Jeremy. Sam is employed as a (computer) software engineer at a multinational company in Belfast.

Sam has been reading the British Reformed Journal for many years, in which time he has contributed several articles. The first BRF conference that he attended was held at Hebron Hall in South Wales in August, 2010, on "The Word of God for Our Generation." This is still what we need today: the fresh and distinctive biblical and Reformed truth of Jesus Christ! May the Triune God use this journal and its new editor to this worthy end!

As well as introducing a new editor, we are glad to announce that the BRF is planning, in God's providence, a conference on "Union With Christ" at Castlewellan Castle, Co. Down, N. Ireland (9-16 July, 2022) with Profs. David J. Engelsma and Brian Huizinga (USA) as our main speakers. Because of Covid and its regulations, it had to be cancelled in 2020 and 2021, so this summer's conference is the most eagerly anticipated one yet. We hope to see many of you there!
