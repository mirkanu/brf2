The British Reformed Journal (BRJ) is the publication of the British Reformed Fellowship, usually with contribution from members, and currently published biannually. It contains doctrinal articles aimed at the propagation of the Reformed faith throughout the British Isles, Europe and abroad.

If you require **hard copies of past issues**, [contact Mary Stewart](mailto:anmstewart@lineone.net?subject=past%20BRJs). While the majority of articles are available online, you may download an **index** of all previous _Journals_ up until the year 2008 by clicking [here](/s/BRJ-Index.doc).

If you are interested in **subscribing**, please visit the link to "[Membership & Subscriptions](/membership)" above. **The newest issue is only available to subscribers.**
