_"The basis of the BRF shall be the inspired, infallible, inerrant Scriptures of the Old and New Testaments as summarized and systemized in the Reformed confessions, specifically the Three Forms of Unity and the Westminster Standards."_

## Doctrinal Basis

1.  The Divine inspired, infallible, and inerrant Holy Scriptures
    
2.  The Doctrine set forth in :
    
    1.  The _Three Forms of Unity_, being:
        
        *   The _Canons of Dordt_,
            
        *   The _Belgic Confession_,
            
        *   The _Heidelberg Catechism_
            
    2.  The _Westminster Standards_
